# Server Authority Boundaries

The learner/teacher/parent/admin clients are not trusted sources for authoritative business outcomes.

## Server-authoritative domains

### Assessment
Final score and attempt status.

### Competition
Eligibility, score, result and ranking.

### Rewards
XP, coins, levels, badges and reward redemption.

### Business
Orders, payments, subscriptions and entitlements.

### Moderation
Restriction, removal and moderation state.

### Automation
Scheduled execution and publication.

All client requests to these domains must be validated server-side before changing authoritative state.
